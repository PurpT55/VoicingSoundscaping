VISION
  A local, professional-grade audiobook and storytelling engine: voices with 80-90 %+ of human
  emotional range (cry, whisper, scream, anger, tired, bored, cheeky, dry humor, peppy, frustrated,
  joy, contentment, sleepy ...), immersive soundscapes and music, mastered to delivery standards.
  No cloud APIs (no ElevenLabs/Suno): open models on your own machine. Purpose: share light,
  good, memorable stories with family, friends and the world.

GOALS (measurable)
  G1 emotional range  >= 80-90 % of the 96-entry taxonomy at native/bank level on the project's backends
  G2 authenticity     listening tests + automatic QA (ASR WER, emotion2vec) on every take
  G3 sound            beds 14-26 dB under speech (measured), no masking, real spaces
  G4 delivery         ACX-compliant master + immersive masters; M4B with chapters, captions
  G5 continuity       any session can resume from this file + the skills in .claude/skills/

COVERAGE (controllability, `vss emotions --markdown`)

  Qwen3 instruct 87.5 % | Step-EditX over IndexTTS 59.4 % (+paralinguistics) | Kokoro/espeak 0 %

DECISIONS (append only)
  2026-10-21 Local-only stack; cloud TTS/music APIs out of scope by design.
  2026-10-21 Backend-agnostic emotion vocabulary; adapters render it (no model syntax in scripts).
  2026-10-21 One venv per heavy model via JSON-lines workers; HTTP servers first-class.
  2026-10-21 Default production voice IndexTTS-2.5 (cloning + disentangled emotion clips); Fish Drama 3/S2 for extremes via routing.
  2026-10-21 Coverage = controllability metric; quality judged by listening + ASR/emotion QA.
  2026-10-21 ACX master drops beds automatically (noise-floor rule); immersive presets keep them.
  2026-10-21 Bed levels default ambience -12 / music -11 dB rel. dialog, ducking 6 dB -> ~20 dB speech-to-bed.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
  - Real GPU models not exercised in this environment (HF downloads blocked; no GPU).
  - Inline <sfx>/emotion shifts split synthesis units on non-inline backends (prosody seam risk).
  - Procedural sounds are background quality; hero SFX want libraries or generators.
  - Emotion coverage is computed from capabilities, not measured perception (see W2.6).
